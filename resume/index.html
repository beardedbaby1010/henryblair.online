ACS NSQIP analysis pipeline — sole author
2026

Roughly 6,000 lines of Python that take twelve years of fixed-width NSQIP Participant Use Files — 397 columns whose names and presence change between years — and produce a harmonized cohort, a live workbook with statistics written as spreadsheet formulas rather than pasted values, publication figures and a written methods guide. Every NSQIP project below was built with it; six abstracts came out of it in one submission cycle.

Vaginal estrogen after the FDA boxed-warning reversal — first author
2026 – present

Interrupted time-series and cohort analyses in the TriNetX US Collaborative Network (42.4 million patients) of prescribing before and after the November 2025 FDA announcement, including negative-control calibration, formulation-specific response, breast cancer survivorship, receptor status and racial equity of uptake. Seven abstracts across two meetings; manuscript V3.

Androgen deprivation therapy in older men — first author
2026 – present

Bone-density screening and treatment intensification in men aged 70 and older with metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer, including a matched analysis separating screening prompted by prior bone disease from screening prompted by ADT itself. Two AUA abstracts; manuscript V7.

Urology resident perceptions of urogynecology — national survey
July – August 2026

National survey of 115 United States urology residents on exposure, mentorship, perceptions of scope, and the timing of fellowship decisions, with thematic content analysis of free-text responses. Three manuscripts drafted; three abstracts submitted to both the AUA and SUFU.

Urogynecologic access and deprivation in Brooklyn (DS4HP) — lead investigator
Fall 2025 – present

Prospective cross-sectional study of 120 women asking whether the Area Deprivation Index — an instrument built where distance means a car — measures what it claims to in a dense urban setting. Also a retrospective outcomes study of surgical utilization, access inequality (PAI) and market concentration (HHI) using ICD-10-PCS mapping and multivariable regression in Stata. Manuscripts in preparation.

AUA Core Curriculum teaching videos
2026

Scripted, filmed and edited two teaching videos — sacral neuromodulation lead placement, and vaginal pessary types and fitting — for procedures residents otherwise learn by observation alone. Submitted to the AUA Core Curriculum, August 31st, 2026.

Medical Student Research Experience — Maimonides Medical Center
Summer 2024

Analyzed adverse event profiles of the SynchroSeal and Vessel Sealer Extend devices used to seal blood vessels in robotic surgery, in the FDA MAUDE database following the March 2022 field safety notice. Presented at the Society of Urologic Robotic Surgeons Focal Therapy conference and at regional surgical meetings.

Personal project. A language-model study assistant that answers Step 1 questions in a fixed high-yield structure and ends every answer at a citation — the actual page in First Aid, AMBOSS or Rx Bricks — rather than reproducing the content. Working prototype.

Emory Autism Center
Spring 2021 – Spring 2023

Examined differences in MRI functional connectivity between the anterior insula and two neural networks — salience and ventral attention — in autistic people, correlating atypicality with symptom presence and severity. Wrote and defended a 50+ page honors thesis; presented at symposia.

Epilepsy research
Summer 2017 – Spring 2019

Ran experiments independently in genetically modified mice studying myeloid cell activation and its impact on status epilepticus, using PCR and gene expression analysis; also worked on traumatic brain injury and post-traumatic epilepsy. Presented repeatedly at symposia and received distinction.
