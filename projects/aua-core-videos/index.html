AUA Core Curriculum instructional videos

Henry M. Blair · Luke Jenusaitis, MD · Allison Polland, MD

Two procedures that residents learn by standing next to someone doing them. The AUA Core Curriculum is where that apprenticeship is written down for everyone; these are the two entries I filmed, scripted and cut.

Status. Both videos produced. Final cuts sit with the AUA for the Core Curriculum; they will be embedded on this page or linked to the AUA's own release.

The question

How do you teach a procedure whose difficulty is entirely in the hands — where to stand, what to feel for, what the wrong answer looks like — to someone who cannot be in the room?

How it is being done

  • Sacral neuromodulation device implantation — lead placement and implantation, filmed stepwise with the anatomy called out as it appears.
  • Pessary fitting — sizing and fitting, including the judgment that decides between sizes rather than only the technique.
  • Scripted against the AUA's Core Curriculum format, filmed in theatre, and cut with captions so a step can be found without watching the whole thing.
