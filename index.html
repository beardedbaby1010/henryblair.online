Urinary retention after Retzius-sparing prostatectomy

Retzius-sparing robotic prostatectomy gives men better early continence, but preserving the anterior compartment may cost postoperative retention — and no study has examined retention as a primary outcome. In ~200 patients I am measuring its incidence and testing predictors: membranous urethral length, intravesical prostatic protrusion, prostate volume, baseline LUTS, PSA, diabetes, frailty, and peri-operative alpha-blocker, 5-ARI and ADT use.

Can women self-estimate their post-void residual?

AUA guidelines require a post-void residual measurement to diagnose overactive bladder — which needs an in-office ultrasound, at odds with the telemedicine pathway the same guidelines endorse. Women could not estimate their own PVR reliably; but incomplete emptying was rare and no woman met criteria for retention, so the in-office requirement may be safely deferred for low-risk women, opening the door to telemedicine.

Retrospective cohort · Maimonides Medical Center

Does neighborhood deprivation predict urogynecologic access in Brooklyn?

A companion overactive-bladder study testing whether the Area Deprivation Index and travel burden relate to access and disease severity — and whether ADI is even valid in a dense, transit-rich city. It did not track access or severity in Brooklyn, likely because public transit blunts the travel barriers seen elsewhere; disadvantaged women were, however, more likely to report urge frequency.

What do patients with nocturia actually want treated?

Nocturia trials fixate on nightly void count, but that may not be what patients care about. This study examines patient-defined treatment goals and how much nighttime urination patients will tolerate — outcomes beyond the void count.
